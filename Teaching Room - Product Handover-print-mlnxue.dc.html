AI Teaching Room

An AI-guided, single-topic interactive lesson experience. A student is walked through one concept end-to-end by an on-screen AI tutor, moving through a fixed sequence of teaching screens that mix narration, hands-on manipulatives, video, and a quiz — with a live chat/voice channel available throughout.

1. Concept & characters

The Teaching Room presents one lesson topic as a linear, narrated journey. The learner advances step by step; each step is a full-screen "teaching mode" with its own layout and interaction. Two AI characters carry the experience:

  • Max — the primary AI tutor. Max narrates every step (text + synthesized voice), poses questions, and reacts to the learner's actions. Max is the character who docks into the chat bar and stays present throughout.
  • Spark — a secondary companion character. Spark appears at specific moments (the intro fly-in, the video pause beat) as an optional co-presence. Spark can be turned off entirely.

2. Screen layout

Every step shares one frame:

  • The stage — the main teaching canvas (title, manipulative, video, quiz, etc.). The canvas is always 16:9. It occupies the full width when the chat is closed and narrows when the chat opens.
  • The chat panel — a right-hand column (372px wide) for the conversation between the learner, Max, and Spark. It is closed by default on every step, including the first.
  • The dock — bottom-left, where Max lives once docked, alongside the Chat and Speak (mic) entry buttons.

Key layout rule

The stage resizes with the chat. Opening the chat reserves the 372px column and shrinks the stage; closing it releases the column back to 0px so the stage uses the full width and shifts to fill the space. When the chat is closed, the Chat / Speak buttons and docked Max float as an overlay over the full-width stage so they stay reachable.

3. The chat & voice panel

  • Closed by default on load and on every step change — the learner opens it themselves.
  • Two entry points from the dock: Chat (opens the panel with the text field focused) and Speak (opens the panel in mic / recording state).
  • While the chat is closed, new tutor messages surface as floating pop-up bubbles above the dock, then auto-dismiss; opening the chat shows the full running transcript.
  • Messages reveal progressively and are narrated aloud (Max, and Spark when enabled) via the browser's speech synthesis.

4. Intro sequence (Step 1)

The first screen is the title card. On load:

  1. Max and Spark appear at their starting position on the board (title area).
  2. Within the first ~5 seconds, they automatically fly to the dock — Max landing bottom-left beside the Chat / Speak pill, Spark flying off.
  3. Only after Max has docked does the first tutor message appear. No message is shown before docking.

5. Lesson flow — the 14 steps

The reference lesson ("Area of a Trapezoid") runs the following fixed sequence. Each step's mode determines its layout and the tab it maps to.

STEP 01Standard

Title — "Area of a trapezoid"

Intro card + mascot dock-in. Max introduces the topic.

STEP 02Teach

Learning objectives

Three objectives reveal one by one, each narrated and highlighted in sequence, ending with a "Got it" confirmation.

STEP 03Applet

Parallelogram manipulative

An embedded interactive: the learner drags the top corner of a parallelogram to deform it into a trapezoid. The system detects interaction, guides toward the goal, and confirms on success.

STEP 04Teach

Definition — "This new shape is a trapezoid"

Defines the trapezoid: a quadrilateral with only one pair of parallel sides. Question + answer options render directly beneath the trapezoid figure (fixed 50px gap).

STEP 05Video

Watch & learn (video)

A lesson video showing how the shape is transformed to find its area. Custom player; includes a scripted pause beat (see §6).

STEP 06Teach

Do & learn — duplicate into a parallelogram

Learner duplicates the trapezoid and rotates the copy so the two together form a parallelogram.

STEP 07Quiz

Shout out loud — recall the formula

Multiple choice: which formula gives the area of a trapezoid? Options: ½×(a+b)×h · (a+b)×h · ½×a×b · a×b×h. Correct: ½×(a+b)×h.

STEP 08Screen

Formula reveal — "That's right!"

Confirms and displays Area = ½ × (a + b) × h, defining a, b (parallel sides) and h (height).

STEP 09Screen

Do & learn — cut & rearrange into a triangle

Learner cuts and rearranges the trapezoid into a triangle, showing the area is preserved.

STEP 10Teach

Math vault — "The area always stays the same"

Reinforces that area depends only on the parallel sides and height, invariant under rearrangement.

STEP 11Applet

Practice — find the missing height

Interactive practice problem; learner works toward the answer.

STEP 12Applet

Practice solved

Worked solution state: Area ÷ average base = 300 ÷ 30 = 10 cm.

STEP 13Teach

Module complete — key ideas

Recap of the three key takeaways and the final formula.

6. Video player (Step 5)

  • Opens as a framed thumbnail centered on the stage, then expands to full-bleed on play.
  • Control bar layout: volume icon + volume slider on the left; skip-back / play-pause / skip-forward grouped in the center; a playback-speed pill on the right (tap to cycle through speeds).
  • Scripted pause beat at 1:09 — a Lottie pause animation plays center-screen; partway through, a Spark message appears. This is the dedicated SparkVideoPause moment.

7. Background feedback

Correct / incorrect actions tint the stage background from the bottom edge using two design tokens:

TokenMeaningCoverage
--success-bg-colorGreen glow on a correct actionBottom 50% of stage height
--fail-bg-colorRed glow on an incorrect actionBottom 50% of stage height

Both are layered gradients (a radial glow rising from the bottom-center plus a linear fade) tuned so the color reads over the bottom half and fades to transparent above it.

9. Requirements changelog (latest)

The behaviors below are the current, confirmed requirements as they stand in v2:

  • Background feedback — success/fail gradients cover the bottom 50% of the stage height (raised from 30%).
  • Question & options placement — render in normal flow directly beneath the trapezoid figure, with a fixed 50px gap (not pinned to the bottom of the window).
  • Chat default — closed by default on every step, including the first.
  • Stage resizing — the stage must resize when the chat opens/closes (full width when closed).
  • Intro dock-then-message — Max & Spark start on the board, auto-dock within ~5s, and the first message appears only after Max docks.
  • Video controls — volume left, transport centered, playback-speed pill right.
  • Video pause beat — Lottie pause animation + Spark message at 1:09 (SparkVideoPause).
