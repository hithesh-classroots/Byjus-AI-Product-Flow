05

Two instruments, always running

A treadmill convinces you it is working because the readout never stops moving. The room needs the same: one instrument for what you have built, one for how hard it is going right now. They must mean different things, or they are decoration.

INSTRUMENT 01
The tier rings
What you have built
{{ r.t }} {{ r.v }} {{ r.chip }}
Each arc closes at your last run's count in that tier.

Three arcs — easy, medium, hard — each closing as correct answers land in that tier. It runs the whole session, so the end-of-run breakdown is just the rings at rest. Nothing new appears at the finish.

INSTRUMENT 02
The pace trace
How hard it is going right now
GEAR 1 · STEADY
{{ g.t }}

A trace that scrolls without ever stopping. Its amplitude is answers-per-minute, its scroll speed and colour are the gear. At a gear change the speed visibly jumps — the instrument tells you before Vyom does.
