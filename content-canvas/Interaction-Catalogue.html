Layer 1 · Behaviour tokens

Behaviour tokens

Every movement on a board is one of these tokens. A token fixes which channels move, for how long, with which easing, along what path, with which sound, and where it ends. Values live once in canvas.css; the token definitions live in components/behaviour-tokens.json; runtime/canvas-interaction.js reads both. Hover a token value to see it.

Layer 3 · Components

Interaction components

The 35 presets, each a composition of behaviour tokens. The board under each one is its reference demo: sample content, page controls. The contract is its entry in interaction-specs.json.

Group 1

Core motions

01

slice

slice(whole, cuts, {material, tool})
Ready

Swipe across a dashed score line, or tap it. The tool runs along the line with a slash trail behind it, and the pieces part the moment each cut lands. Pieces keep their size.

Built fromdrawglidemake-roomSpecinteraction-specs.json → slice

Phases

  1. Offer: dashed score lines, --cc-base 2.5
  2. Cut: the tool travels the line; a tapered slash trail follows and closes behind it
  3. Part: after every cut, the pieces either side open by 12 along the cut

Timing

  • Knife 520ms, wheel 750ms, scissors --cc-draw; ease-in-out
  • Trail closes 160ms ease-out
  • Part --cc-morph ease-in-out; chocolate --cc-enter ease-out

Sound

Knife: rising air swoosh, blade hiss and a short metallic ring. Scissors: one snip per blade close. Wheel: rolling rumble with crust crunch. Each material adds its contact sound: crisp paper, soft pizza, chocolate snap.

02

fold

fold(sheet, axis, {material})
Ready

Tap the sheet or the dashed line to fold; tap again to unfold. Or drag the flap across the line: let go past halfway and it completes, before halfway and it falls back.

Built fromliftsettledrawSpecinteraction-specs.json → fold

Phases

  1. Crease: dashed axis
  2. Turn: the flap rotates 180° toward the viewer; its reverse side shows past 90°
  3. Land: the crease stays as a line, folded or open

Timing

  • Turn --cc-draw ease-in-out
  • Released drag finishes in proportion to the angle left, ease-out
  • Depth cue: black layer up to 12% on the flap, 10% under it

Sound

Air swoosh while turning. Landing: a crisp crease tick for paper, a low thud for card.

03

jump

jump(token, from, to, {step})
At 2

A move from A to B as unit hops or one leap. The trail stays, labelled at its apex. Tap any number to leap there.

Built fromhoparcdrawfade-inSpecinteraction-specs.json → jump

Phases

  1. Take-off
  2. Arc: token and trail share one eased progress, so it stays on its line
  3. Land: step label fades in at the apex

Timing

  • Hop --cc-beat, apex ½ × width (28–64)
  • Leap --cc-draw, apex 0.35 × width (48–140)
  • Label --cc-enter

Sound

Take-off glide and a landing tap, the same pitch on every hop.

04

branch

branch(source, children, {direction})
Closed

What can open is a button (it stands on an edge and carries a chevron). What can't is a flat surface. Tap a button to open its branch; tap it again to close it. Closing the source closes everything under it.

Built fromdrawglidefade-inrestSpecinteraction-specs.json → branch

Phases

  1. Open: chevron turns 90°, connectors draw, children travel out of the parent
  2. Second level opens from its button the same way; that button takes selected and its path lights
  3. Close: reverse order, deepest level first, back into the parent

Timing

  • Children and connectors --cc-enter ease-out
  • One --cc-stagger between siblings
  • Max 4 children, 2 levels

Sound

Open: rising air plus a tick per child arriving. Close: falling air.

05

reorder

reorder(list, {judge})
Drag a card

Pick a card up: it lifts, tilts from where you hold it, and sways as you move it, then springs level when you drop it. Arrow keys work on a focused card.

Built fromliftmake-roomsettlejudgeSpecinteraction-specs.json → reorder

Phases

  1. Lift: rises 8px, tilts up to 2.5° about the grab point, deep shadow, a sheen crosses it
  2. Carry: follows 1:1; swings with speed (±4°); others make room
  3. Drop: settles, springs level with one small overshoot
  4. Check marks in place; Reveal is the last rung

Timing

  • Make room --cc-fast ease-out
  • Settle --cc-enter ease-out; tilt on a spring
  • Reveal --cc-morph

Sound

Lift glide, a tick per slot passed, a soft drop. Judgement uses the existing correct / wrong tones.

10

Rolled

Built fromglidefade-inSpecinteraction-specs.json → roll

  1. Roll travels; the sheet appears behind it
  2. The roll's surface turns as it moves

11

move

move(object, to, {principles})
At A

Position change with the principles of motion, each switchable so you can see what it adds. Tap anywhere on the board to send the block there, or drag it. Send or drop it in the no-go zone and it is refused: it turns to the wrong state, shakes, and travels back to where it started. Squash and stretch is off by default because it scales the object, which the system currently forbids.

Built fromglidearcsettlereturn-homeSpecinteraction-specs.json → move

Principles

  • Slow in and out: ease-in-out, not linear
  • Arc: curved path, bowing up 28% of the distance
  • Anticipation: 14px back-step, 160ms
  • Follow-through: 7% overshoot, settles
  • Secondary action: the tag swings on a spring
  • Squash and stretch: along the velocity (breaks no-scale)
  • No-go: zone border goes solid wrong while the object is over it; on arrival, wrong hue and cross, a --cc-morph shake, 260ms pause, then back to the start over --cc-morph; the mark clears as it leaves

Timing

  • --cc-morph under 300px, --cc-draw beyond
  • Show spacing draws a ghost every 45ms: close ghosts are slow, far apart are fast

Sound

Air whoosh that follows the speed; a soft landing tap. Refused: a low thunk, then a falling whoosh on the way back.

12

shade

shade(parts, {container, mode})
Nothing shaded

Parts inside a whole take shading from where they belong: a pizza slice fills outward from the centre, a grid square fills up from its base, a bottle fills like liquid to a level. Tap a part to shade or clear it. Drag across several parts to paint them in one stroke; a drag that starts on a shaded part clears instead. The count updates beside the whole.

Built fromshade-partcount-badgeSpecinteraction-specs.json → shade

Phases

  1. Tap or drag over a part
  2. Fill travels into the part along its own direction: centre outward (sector), bottom up (square), rising level (container)
  3. The count beside the whole names the result; tapping a shaded part clears it the same way back

Timing

  • One part --cc-enter ease-out
  • Demo shades one part per --cc-beat, like count()
  • Liquid --cc-beat per part, up to 1800ms
  • Shaded colour --cc-fill2; partitions --cc-ink

Sound

Sector: a soft brush. Square: a small tick, same pitch each time. Bottle: a pour that rises while filling and falls while draining.

Group 2

Maths behaviours

Standard ways a maths idea moves on the board: placing, balancing, sharing, regrouping, constructing, transforming, plotting, measuring, building, zooming, folding, charting and matching.

13

drag to target

dragTo(item, targets, {judge})
Ready

Drag a number onto the line. Near a slot it lights and the chip snaps there. Right slot: it stays, marked correct. Wrong slot: marked wrong, shakes, goes back to the tray. Dropped nowhere: it just goes back, no mark.

Built fromliftsnapjudgereturn-homeSpecinteraction-specs.json → dragTo

Phases

  1. Lift: edge plus raised shadow; follows 1:1
  2. Hover a target within 48px: slot lights (one at a time), tick
  3. Drop: snap to the slot; judge; wrong returns home, off-target returns without a mark

Timing

  • Snap --cc-fast ease-out
  • Wrong: shake --cc-morph, 260ms hold, return --cc-morph; mark clears 40% of the way
  • Off-target return --cc-morph

Sound

Lift glide, a tick per target lit, a click on snap, then the correct or wrong tones. Returns get a falling whoosh.

balance and equation moves

balance(left, right) · moveTerm(eq, term)
Ready

The scale and the equation change together. Taking 3 from both sides keeps the beam level while +3 crosses the equals sign and turns into −3, then 11 and −3 join into 8. Taking from one side only tips the beam and turns = into ≠.

Built fromleavesettlearcswap-glyphconvergeSpecinteraction-specs.json → balance

Phases

  1. Items leave a pan: rise 24px and fade, one stagger apart
  2. Beam settles on a spring to its new angle (±14° max)
  3. A term travels across =; its sign flips halfway; joined terms travel together, fade, and their result appears

Timing

  • Item leave --cc-enter, --cc-stagger apart
  • Term travel --cc-morph; sign swap --cc-fast
  • Combine --cc-morph, result --cc-enter

Sound

A pop per item, a wooden creak when the beam moves, a whoosh as a term crosses, a tick when terms combine.

18

transformations

translate · reflect · dilate · fit
Ready

Each transformation leaves the original as a ghost. Translation follows a drawn vector. Reflection turns the shape over the mirror line. Dilation draws rays from O, then grows. Test fit turns and slides a copy onto a target to show congruence.

Built fromdrawglidefade-inSpecinteraction-specs.json → transform

Phases

  1. Show the rule first: vector, mirror line, or rays from O
  2. Move the shape; labels turn to A′ B′ C′ halfway

Timing

  • Vector, rays --cc-morph
  • Translate --cc-morph; reflect, dilate --cc-draw
  • Dilation is the one scale in the system: it is the maths (needs a rule exception)

Sound

Whoosh on travel, a card whip on reflect, a rising glide on dilate, the correct tones on a fit.

35

step-by-step working

derive(line) · column(op)
Ready

Each line is made from the one before it. Six kinds of working: factorising, solving, column addition, long division, HCF and LCM by the ladder, and matrix multiplication. One rule never bends: in column working, every digit that comes to rest sits in its own place-value column, digit by digit; a multi-digit result is written as separate digits, never as one block centred between columns. The whole line copies down, then the part being worked on gets a focus box and changes in place while its neighbours make room. Terms that are the same thing share a colour, a common factor drops out of both terms and merges into one, and terms cross the equals sign. In column addition, the digits drop into the answer row. A sum too big for its place sits small and unsteady until the ten leaves for the next column, then the ones digit grows to full size.

Built fromcopy-downfocus-framesplit-openconvergearcswap-glyphtintstrikeanswer-framedrawSpecinteraction-specs.json → steps

Phases

  1. Copy down: the line slides down as the new working line; the old one dims
  2. Focus: a box goes round the part being changed and follows it as it grows
  3. Rewrite in place: a term splits (9 becomes 3 × 3) or merges (3 × 2 becomes 6) while neighbours travel to make room
  4. Same thing, same colour: shared factors light together, then copies drop and merge into one
  5. Cross the equals: a term travels to the other side and its operation flips halfway
  6. Place value is fixed: a digit at rest is always centred on its column (ones under ones, tens under tens), including products, remainders and carries
  7. Long division: product and remainder form where they are written; the next digit drops down along a dotted path
  8. HCF and LCM: numbers step down the ladder, changing as they go; the left side gives the HCF, the L shape gives the LCM
  9. Matrices: a row band and a column band cross; pairs meet as products, and the products meet in the cell
  10. Carry: copies of the column's digits drop into the answer slot; each copy fades as it crosses the line and the sum appears just below it; an overfull sum shows at 55% size and trembles gently, its ten arcs up to the next column, and the ones digit grows to full size

Timing

  • Copy down, rewrite, cross --cc-morph
  • Focus box moves --cc-enter
  • Merge: travel --cc-morph, result appears halfway
  • Overfull shake 900ms; carry --cc-morph; settle --cc-morph with a small overshoot

Sound

A tick per symbol typed, a whoosh when a line or term travels, a pop when something splits or merges, a drop when it lands. An overfull sum rattles while it shakes. No side labels: the movement carries the meaning, and the status chip names each step for the reader.
